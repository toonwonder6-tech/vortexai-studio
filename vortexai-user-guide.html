Welcome to VortexAI Studio

VortexAI Studio is a complete, production-ready AI video production platform that puts a full creative studio in your browser. Every feature is 100% free for every registered user — there are no subscriptions, no credit packs, no paywalls, and no premium tiers. The entire platform is powered exclusively by Z.ai, the central AI engine, whose API credentials are configured by the platform owner on the server. You never need to purchase your own API key or connect an external AI account.

The studio provides a complete video production pipeline: text-to-video, image-to-video, multi-reference video generation, AI image creation and editing, AI Director for one-click automated productions, script and storyboard generation, AI voiceovers, a browser-based video editor with server-side FFmpeg rendering, batch generation, reusable character and brand libraries, and a comprehensive administrator control panel.

This guide walks you through every feature, every workflow, and every menu — from creating your first account to producing a complete multi-scene animated video with voiceover and editing. It also covers the platform's fair-use limits, troubleshooting common errors, and honest capability notes about what AI video generation can and cannot do today.

Free Platform Promise
All registered users receive equal access to every AI tool. The administrator can configure reasonable fair-use limits (daily generation caps, concurrency, storage) to protect the owner's API balance, but no user is ever asked to pay. Free access does not mean unlimited API usage — it means equal, free access to the available functionality.

Chapter 07

AI Director — One-Click Production

AI Director is the platform's signature feature. Enter a simple concept, and the AI automatically plans, scripts, storyboards, generates reference images, produces voiceovers, creates individual video clips, and assembles them into a final production. It's a full automated pipeline in four stages.

The Four-Stage Pipeline
  1. Concept — Enter your idea and pick a style preset (Cinematic, Product Ad, Social Media, etc.). The AI analyzes your concept and develops a full production plan with title, logline, style, and scene breakdown.
  2. Plan & Script — Review the AI-generated plan: scene headings, narration, image prompts, and video prompts — all editable inline. Tweak any field before generation. Regenerate the plan or proceed to generation.
  3. Asset Generation — For each scene, the pipeline runs sequentially: generates a reference image, creates a voiceover (TTS), then submits a video generation job using the image as the first frame. Live status indicators show each scene's progress.
  4. Assemble & Review — Once all scenes complete, review them in a filmstrip view. Play each scene, then open the Video Editor to combine them into one final video with transitions. Save the project to your dashboard.
Pause & Resume
The pipeline supports Pause and Resume controls. If you need to step away or manage the Z.ai rate limit, pause the pipeline at any point — it will finish the current scene and wait. Resume when ready. Individual scenes can also be retried if they fail.
Honest Capability Note
AI Director produces a creative video inspired by your concept. The Z.ai cogvideox model cannot guarantee that multiple distinct characters maintain exact identity, clothing, and appearance across scenes — no model in this class can do multi-character identity preservation from a text prompt. Each scene will be visually inspired by your descriptions but not pixel-perfect consistent.

Chapter 15

Troubleshooting

Error Code 429 — Rate Limit
Z.ai Rate Limit
Z.ai's video endpoint has a strict rate limit (roughly 1 request every 5–10 minutes). Image and text endpoints are more lenient. If you see "Z.ai is rate-limiting video requests," wait a few minutes and try again. The platform automatically retries once with a 2-minute backoff.
Error Code 1214 — Parameter Rejected

This means Z.ai rejected a parameter in your request. Common causes: sending MP3 format to TTS (use WAV instead), or sending size/duration/fps/quality parameters that the model doesn't support. The platform handles this automatically — advanced parameters are off by default, and TTS defaults to WAV.

Error Code 1210 — API Parameter Error

Usually caused by image-to-video with a localhost URL (Z.ai's servers can't reach localhost). The platform automatically converts local images to base64 data URIs and resizes them to 1024px before sending. If you still see this, try a different reference image.

502 Bad Gateway

This was a previous issue with image generation timing out at the gateway. It has been fixed — image generation now runs asynchronously (returns instantly, polls for completion). If you encounter a 502 on any other endpoint, it likely means the operation took longer than 30 seconds; wait a moment and retry.

Stuck "Processing" Jobs

If a video job stays in "processing" for over an hour, it likely failed silently due to rate limits. Use the "Clean up failed" button in the Video Generator to remove stuck and failed jobs. Jobs stuck for over an hour are automatically cleaned up by the cleanup endpoint.

Videos Not Appearing

All completed videos are saved to your Asset Library and appear in the Video Generator's "Your generations" panel. If a video is missing, check the Asset Library (filter by Videos) or My Projects. The dashboard's "Recent generations" also shows your latest completed jobs with thumbnails.

Chapter 17

Honest Capability Notes

VortexAI Studio is built on a principle of honesty: we never claim capabilities the AI cannot deliver, and we never show fake results. Here's what the Z.ai engine can and cannot do today, so you can set realistic expectations.

What the AI Can Do
Video Generation

Generate high-quality cinematic clips from text prompts, animate reference images as first frames, produce 4–10 second clips with optional audio, and follow camera, lighting, environment, and motion instructions well.

Image Generation

Create detailed images in 7 aspect ratios, edit existing images with natural-language instructions, and produce results in 16 visual styles from cinematic to pixel art.

Text & Voice

Write structured scripts and storyboards, enhance prompts professionally, generate natural voiceovers in 7 voices, and transcribe audio to text.

What the AI Cannot Do
Multi-Character Identity Lock
The Z.ai cogvideox model cannot guarantee that multiple distinct characters (e.g., 6 different characters in one scene) maintain exact identity, clothing, and appearance throughout a generated video. No model in this class can do multi-character identity preservation from a text prompt or single reference image. The platform will never claim otherwise.

Character consistency using a single reference image as the first frame works for the opening frame, but the model may drift as motion begins. For best results, use shorter clips (4–6 seconds), keep character counts low, and use the Video Editor to combine multiple short clips rather than expecting one long generation to stay consistent.

No Fake Results
Every video, image, and voiceover you see in the platform is genuinely generated by Z.ai. There are no mockups, no stock footage, and no placeholder content. If a generation fails, you'll see a clear error — never a fake success.
Chapter 18

Music Video Studio

The Music Video Studio is a complete end-to-end workflow for turning any song into a cinematic music video. Upload a song, transcribe lyrics, plan scenes around the music, generate visual clips, and render a final video with the song as the continuous master audio track — all in five guided steps.

The Five-Step Workflow
  1. Upload Song — Upload an audio file (MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A) or choose from your Asset Library. Select a mode: Music Montage (visuals cut to the music) or Singing Characters (characters appear on screen, but mouths will NOT sync to vocals — this is a model limitation).
  2. Analyze Song — The platform probes the song's duration and attempts ASR transcription. Z.ai's ASR is optimized for speech, not music, so transcription may fail — in that case, paste lyrics manually. Detected timings are estimates and must be reviewed.
  3. Edit Lyrics — An editable lyric table with per-line start/end timestamps. Import or export SRT subtitles. Timings from ASR are labeled "ESTIMATED" in amber — review and correct them manually before rendering.
  4. Plan Scenes — The AI auto-generates 8-second scenes (with a shorter final scene) based on your lyrics and song duration. Each scene has a visual prompt, camera direction, and optional character assignment. Generate storyboard images and video clips per scene.
  5. Preview & Export — The platform checks if your scenes cover the full song duration and warns about gaps. Choose resolution, transitions, lyric overlays, and render. The master song plays continuously as the audio track.
Honest Lip-Sync Note
Z.ai's cogvideox model does NOT support audio-conditioned mouth or beak animation. "Singing Characters" mode produces music montage with characters present, but their mouths will NOT sync to vocals. The platform never labels results as "lip-synced" or "synchronized." This is a model limitation, not a bug — the UI clearly states "NOT SUPPORTED" throughout.
Duration Mismatch Detection

Before rendering, the platform compares your scene coverage to the song duration. If your scenes cover only part of the song (e.g., an 8-second clip for a 2:59 song), you'll see a prominent warning: "Your scenes cover only Xs of a Ys song. The final video will have gaps." You can choose to render the excerpt or go back and generate more clips.

SRT Import & Export

The lyric editor supports full SRT subtitle import and export. Import an existing SRT file to populate lyrics with verified timestamps, or export your edited lyrics as SRT for use in other tools. This is the recommended way to get accurate timing — ASR estimates are approximate only.

Chapter 19

Video Extend — Seamless Continuation

The Extend feature takes the last frame of an existing video and uses it as the first frame of a new generation — creating a seamless continuation, just like Google Veo's video extension. Since Z.ai cogvideox supports 5s or 10s clips, Extend lets you build longer videos by chaining clips together.

How to Extend a Video
  1. Generate a video — Create a 5s or 10s clip in the AI Video Generator
  2. Click "Extend" — The button appears on every completed video card. The last frame is extracted automatically using FFmpeg.
  3. Write what happens next — Describe the continuation (e.g., "the camera slowly pans up to reveal a starry sky")
  4. Generate — The new video starts where the old one ended
  5. Combine — Use the Video Editor to concatenate the original + extension into one seamless video
Building 15-Second Videos
Generate a 10s clip → Extend with a 5s continuation → Combine in the Video Editor. Result: a seamless 15-second video. Chain multiple extensions for even longer videos.

Updated Troubleshooting

Modal Won't Close

If a preview dialog appears stuck, try: (1) click the X button at the top-right, (2) press the Escape key, or (3) click outside the dialog on the dark overlay. All three methods close the dialog. If none work, reload the page (F5).

Blank Page / "Loading Studio…"

If the page shows "Loading studio…" indefinitely, the dev server may have restarted after a code change. Press F5 (or Cmd+R on Mac) to reload. The server recovers automatically — the crash protection prevents permanent death.

Videos Not Loading (Blank Thumbnails)

If video thumbnails appear blank: (1) check the Asset Library — if videos are missing, they may have been lost during a server restart, (2) try regenerating the video, (3) the platform uses HTTP Range requests for video streaming — if your browser doesn't support Range, videos won't play. All modern browsers support this.

Music Video — ASR Can't Transcribe

Z.ai's ASR is optimized for speech, not music. If you see "ASR could not transcribe this audio," that's expected for songs. Paste your lyrics manually in the Edit Lyrics step, or import an SRT file with verified timestamps.

Music Video — Duration Mismatch Warning

If you see "Your scenes cover only Xs of a Ys song," you need to generate more video clips for your scenes. Each scene needs a video clip (generated or uploaded). Scenes without clips show a black placeholder during rendering.

Video Generation — Z.ai Rate Limit (429)

Z.ai's video endpoint has a strict rate limit (~1 request every 5-10 minutes). The platform auto-retries in the background with 60-second waits (up to 5 attempts). If you see "Z.ai is rate-limiting video requests," wait a few minutes and try again.

Video Generation — Parameter Rejected (1214)

The platform automatically strips parameters that Z.ai rejects and retries with a minimal body. If you still see this error, try a shorter prompt (under 300 characters) or disable style/realism options.

Render Fails (404 or 502)

If the Video Editor render returns 404, ensure you have clips on the timeline. If you get 502, the render took too long — try fewer clips or lower resolution. The render endpoint has been fixed to the correct path (/api/editor/render).

TTS Fails (1214 — MP3 Not Supported)

Z.ai's TTS supports WAV and PCM only — MP3 is rejected. The platform defaults to WAV. If you see this error, ensure the format dropdown shows "WAV (recommended)" — MP3 is disabled in the UI.

Clean Up Failed Jobs

If the "Your generations" panel has failed/stuck jobs, click the "Clean up failed" button (appears automatically when failed jobs exist). This removes failed and expired jobs, plus jobs stuck in "processing" for over 1 hour.

Lip-Sync — Honest Capability Guide

This chapter provides a thorough, honest explanation of what the platform can and cannot do regarding character lip-sync in music videos. The platform follows a strict policy: never claim capabilities the AI model cannot deliver.

What "Lip-Sync" Means

True lip-sync (lip synchronization) means a character's mouth shapes and movements precisely match the vocal sounds being sung — including phonemes (consonant/vowel shapes), held notes, pauses, syllable timing, and emotional expression. This requires audio-conditioned facial animation — a model that takes an audio track as input and drives mouth shapes from the audio waveform.

What Z.ai cogvideox Supports

Z.ai's cogvideox-2 video model accepts:

  • Text prompts (text-to-video)
  • Reference images (image-to-video, multi-reference)
  • Optional audio generation (ambient sound effects, NOT vocal-driven animation)
  • Quality mode (speed vs quality)

It does NOT accept:

  • Audio tracks for facial animation conditioning
  • Phoneme-level timing data
  • Lyric-to-mouth-shape mapping
  • Per-character singing instructions
The Hard Truth
Accurate, continuous lip-sync is NOT possible with Z.ai's cogvideox model. The model cannot take your song's audio and drive character mouth movements from it. No parameter, configuration, or workaround can make the model sync mouths to vocals. This is a fundamental model limitation, not a bug or missing feature.
What the Platform Does Instead

The Music Video Studio offers two modes, both honestly labeled:

Music Montage Mode (Recommended)

Visuals are cut to the music with scene transitions. Characters appear in scenes with gestures and expressions matching the mood, but their mouths do NOT sync to vocals. Lyric captions (subtitles) are burned into the video at user-specified timestamps. The song plays as the continuous master audio track at original speed and pitch. This is the honest, production-ready approach.

Singing Characters Mode (Limited)

Characters appear on screen as if performing, but the platform clearly labels every scene with "LIP-SYNC NOT SUPPORTED" in red. The result is visually similar to Music Montage — characters are present, but mouths move randomly or stay static. The platform never labels this as "synchronized" or "lip-synced."

Why Not Add External Lip-Sync Tools?

Tools like Wav2Lip, SadTalker, and MuseTalk can perform audio-driven facial animation. However, adding these would violate the platform's core rules:

  • "Z.ai must be the exclusive external AI provider" — no other AI APIs
  • "No additional paid AI provider integrations" — no external dependencies
  • "Do not fabricate capabilities" — don't claim sync that didn't happen
Recommended Workflow for Music Videos
  1. Upload your song — Original speed, original pitch, full duration
  2. Paste lyrics manually — ASR can't transcribe music; paste your lyrics in the Edit Lyrics step
  3. Set timestamps manually or import SRT — Use an SRT file for verified timing
  4. Plan 8-second scenes — The AI generates visual prompts matching each lyric section
  5. Generate clips per scene — Use 3D Animation style preset for child-friendly visuals
  6. Enable lyric overlays — Burn in subtitle captions at each scene's timestamp
  7. Render with transitions — Use fade or dissolve transitions between scenes
  8. Result — A polished music video with your song as the soundtrack, characters in each scene, and synced lyric captions — honestly labeled as music montage
